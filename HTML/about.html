About Stackly

We test what you carry.

A six-person repair bench that grew into a storefront. Everything we sell has been bent, heated, dropped and drained first.

Shop tested gear Move your cursor to light the bench
Our one rule

Stackly began on a repair bench with one rule: if we would not carry it, we would not sell it. Every cable is bent ten thousand times, every charger is cooked for a full day, and every earbud is dropped before it ever reaches your hands.

Seven years

From one bench to one drop

  1. 2019

    The bench

    One desk, one soldering iron and a queue of cracked screens from friends.

  2. 2021

    The lab

    We built a rig that drains, heats and drops gear so the results stop being opinions.

  3. 2024

    The doorstep

    Free pickup and drop-off launched, so a repair no longer meant a trip across town.

  4. 2026

    The drop

    Over 400 tested accessories, 58 brands and one clean storefront.
